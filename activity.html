About Basketball

Basketball is played by two teams, usually with five players on the court for each team.

The main goal is to score more points than the opposing team.

Basic Basketball Rules

  1. Players must dribble while moving with the ball.
  2. Players cannot intentionally carry the ball.
  3. A team must try to score in the opponent's basket.
  4. Physical fouls are not allowed.
  5. The team with more points wins the game.
